Statusly Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Statusly helps you view, save, organize and share WhatsApp and WhatsApp Business status photos and videos on your Android device.

Regional rewards availability

Mobile-data reward offers are currently available only to eligible users in Nigeria. The App checks approximate country using your IP address. Users outside the eligible region do not see reward offers or campaign popups.

For eligible users, rewards device registration may begin automatically when the App starts, before joining an offer. Additional information is processed for referrals, qualifying activity and reward claims as explained below.

Advertising is separate from rewards eligibility. Advertising-enabled versions may display ads to users outside Nigeria as well as users in Nigeria. Your selected app language does not determine your country or reward eligibility.

Features and data processing depend on the version of Statusly installed and the features available to you.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Iszy_Man, the developer of Statusly, handles information when you use the Statusly mobile application ("Statusly" or "the App").

Statusly processes status photos and videos locally on your device. You do not need a username or password to use its core saving features.

Rewards-enabled versions use a server-side device profile to record participation, referrals, qualifying activity and reward claims. Advertising-enabled versions also use Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform to support advertising and manage applicable privacy choices.

Not requiring a conventional login does not mean that no information is collected.

2. Information Statusly Accesses

Statusly accesses photos and videos in supported WhatsApp status locations when you grant access or select the relevant folder through Android's file-selection system.

This access supports displaying, viewing and saving status media. Depending on your device and installed applications, Statusly supports WhatsApp and WhatsApp Business folders.

3. How Statusly Uses Accessed Media

Statusly uses accessible media to provide features including:

  • Displaying and previewing status photos and videos.
  • Saving selected media to your device.
  • Organizing and viewing saved media.
  • Sharing or reposting media when you choose.

Statusly does not upload your status photos or videos to its rewards server. For rewards qualification, the App may send a content hash and metadata about a successfully saved status. This metadata is separate from the photo or video.

4. Information Processed for Rewards and Support

Depending on your eligibility, installed app version and features used, Statusly processes:

  • Device identification. The App sends your Android ID to our API. The API derives a hashed device identifier for rewards registration, authentication and fraud prevention. Hashing makes this information pseudonymous, not anonymous.
  • Authentication information. Rewards device tokens are stored using the App's secure-storage mechanism. Token hashes and expiry information are maintained by the API.
  • Referral information. This includes referral codes, installation attribution obtained through Google Play when available, links between inviter and invitee devices, and referral qualification records.
  • Qualifying-save information. This includes content hashes, media type, source application category, file size and activity timestamps. It supports task progress and duplicate-save detection.
  • Campaign and reward records. This includes offers joined, task progress, earned rewards, recurring eligibility and redemption history.
  • Redemption information. When you claim mobile data, we process the destination phone number, selected network, reward or bundle, transaction references, processing messages and delivery status.
  • Phone-number reservation information. We retain a phone-number hash, limited number details and its association with a rewards device to prevent the same number being reserved by different devices. Full destination numbers are also processed in redemption records.
  • Connection and country information. Our servers and hosting infrastructure receive your IP address and technical request information. Operational logs may contain timestamps and error information. We use an IP-country database to estimate your country for regional eligibility.
  • Support correspondence. If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you include in your message.

Statusly does not request your WhatsApp password, private chat messages, contacts, bank-card details or GPS location for these features. A phone number is required when requesting delivery of a mobile-data reward.

Advertising-related information is described in section 9. Rewards device identification and advertising identifiers serve different purposes.

5. Local Storage and App Preferences

Statusly stores preferences such as your selected language, onboarding status, selected WhatsApp source and folder organization on your device.

Rewards authentication tokens use the App's secure-storage mechanism. Availability results and other information may be cached temporarily in memory to reduce repeated requests.

Google advertising and consent components may also store or access information on your device, including identifiers, consent status and privacy preferences, according to their configuration and applicable choices.

Server-side records are separate from local preferences and can remain after the App is uninstalled.

6. Saved Photos and Videos

Saved photos and videos are stored on your device using Android's storage system. Statusly does not keep a server-side copy of saved status media as part of its normal operation.

Saved files remain until you delete them through Statusly, your gallery, a file manager or other available tools.

7. Sharing Information With Third Parties

Statusly shares information where needed to operate its features, deliver rewards, support advertising, manage privacy choices and protect against abuse.

  • Hosting and infrastructure providers process information needed to operate and store API records.
  • VTpass and fulfilment providers receive relevant redemption details, including the destination number, network, selected bundle and transaction reference, to deliver mobile data and check transaction status.
  • Google and applicable advertising partners process advertising and consent information as explained in section 9. Where a consent message is shown, it provides information about relevant purposes and participating partners.
  • Apps and services you select for sharing receive the content or link you choose to share through Android. Referral links contain a referral code.

Information may also be disclosed where necessary to comply with applicable legal obligations or valid legal requests.

8. Third-Party Services and Country Detection

Statusly uses third-party software and services for Google Play installation attribution, app updates, API hosting, VTpass reward fulfilment, Google AdMob advertising and Google UMP privacy-choice management.

Country detection for rewards uses a DB-IP database stored on our server. This lookup does not send each visitor's IP address to an external DB-IP lookup API. It is separate from Google's advertising-related processing of IP addresses.

IP-country detection is approximate. It does not establish nationality or exact physical location. VPNs, travel and network routing can affect results.

Providers may process information on infrastructure outside your country. Their involvement depends on the feature used, configuration and applicable choices.

9. Advertising and Privacy Choices

Google AdMob

Advertising-enabled versions of Statusly display banner advertising through Google AdMob. Ads help support the operation and development of the App.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares information for advertising, measurement, analytics and fraud prevention. Depending on configuration and available privacy choices, this can include:

  • IP addresses, which may be used to estimate approximate location.
  • Advertising identifiers, app set identifiers and other identifiers described in Google's SDK disclosures.
  • App and advertising interactions, such as app launches, taps, ad impressions and video views.
  • Diagnostic and performance information concerning the App and advertising SDK.

The App does not request GPS permission for its current banner-ad implementation. Approximate location derived from an IP address is different from GPS-based location.

Consent and advertising choices

Statusly uses Google's User Messaging Platform to request and manage privacy choices where applicable, including relevant choices for users in the European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland.

Ads may be personalized, non-personalized or limited, or unavailable, depending on your choices, applicable requirements and Google's serving rules. Declining personalized advertising does not necessarily remove all ads or stop all processing needed for permitted advertising, measurement, security or fraud prevention.

Where required, a privacy-options entry in the App's Settings lets you reopen the privacy form and change or withdraw applicable choices. Android also provides advertising-ID controls; available options depend on your device and Android version.

Information about Google's processing and controls is available at:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps and sites that use its services
  • Google My Ad Center

Advertising and Statusly rewards

Statusly campaign popups announce available reward offers and are separate from third-party ads. The current task-based rewards do not require you to view, click or interact with advertisements.

10. Data Security

We use access controls, device authentication and hashed identifiers to help protect rewards information. Hashing does not make identifiers anonymous.

The hosted rewards API uses HTTPS. Google's Mobile Ads SDK uses encrypted transport for the data it transmits. Local development and sandbox testing are separate from public production operation.

No electronic storage system or transmission method is completely secure. Use your device's security features to protect saved files and app access.

11. Data Retention, Deletion and Your Choices

We retain server-side rewards profiles and related records while needed for participation, task progress, referral qualification, delivery, support, fraud prevention and applicable legal obligations.

Uninstalling Statusly or clearing app data may remove local settings and authentication information, depending on Android and backup settings. It does not automatically delete server-side rewards records or make a previously recognized device eligible as a new referral.

You can request access to, correction of or deletion of information we hold about your rewards device or transactions by emailing iszifyaws@gmail.com . We will consider requests and other applicable privacy rights under the laws that apply to you.

We may need limited information to verify that a request concerns your device or transactions. Do not send passwords or authentication tokens.

Limited transaction records, device identifiers or phone-number reservation records may need to be retained for fraud prevention, disputes or legal obligations. Where retention is necessary, we will explain the relevant reason when responding. Information should not be retained longer than necessary for its applicable purpose.

Google and other providers handle their own records under their policies. A deletion request to Statusly does not automatically delete information held independently by those providers. Use the provider's privacy controls or request procedures where applicable. Changing advertising consent does not automatically delete earlier records.

Saved media can be deleted separately using Statusly, your gallery or file-management tools.

12. Children's Privacy

Statusly is not specifically designed for children. Depending on the app version and features available, processing may include rewards identifiers, activity information and advertising-related data.

If you believe information about a child has been collected inappropriately, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.
