Statusly Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Statusly helps you view, save, organize and share WhatsApp status photos and videos on your Android device.

Regional rewards availability

Mobile-data reward offers are currently available only to eligible users in Nigeria. The App checks approximate country using your IP address to determine availability. Users outside the eligible region do not see reward offers or campaign popups.

For eligible users, rewards device registration may begin automatically when the App starts, before joining an offer. Additional information is processed for referrals, qualifying activity and reward claims as explained below.

Your selected app language does not determine your country or reward eligibility. Features and data processing also depend on the version of Statusly installed on your device.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Statusly, provided by Iszy_Man, handles information when you use the Statusly mobile application.

Statusly processes status photos and videos locally on your device. You do not need to create a username or password to use its core saving features.

Rewards-enabled versions also use a server-side device profile to record participation, referrals, qualifying activity and reward claims. Not requiring a conventional login does not mean that no information is collected.

2. Information Statusly Accesses

Statusly accesses photos and videos in supported WhatsApp status locations when you grant access or select the relevant folder through Android's file-selection system.

This access supports displaying, viewing and saving status media. Depending on your device and installed applications, Statusly supports WhatsApp and WhatsApp Business folders.

3. How Statusly Uses Accessed Media

Statusly uses accessible media to provide features including:

  • Displaying and previewing status photos and videos.
  • Saving selected media to your device.
  • Organizing and viewing saved media.
  • Sharing or reposting media when you choose to do so.

Statusly does not upload your status photos or videos to its rewards server. For rewards qualification, the App may send a content hash and metadata about a successfully saved status. This metadata is separate from the photo or video.

4. Information Processed for Rewards and Support

Depending on your eligibility, installed app version and features used, Statusly processes:

  • Device identification. The App sends your Android ID to our API. The API derives a hashed device identifier used for rewards registration, authentication and fraud prevention. Hashing makes this information pseudonymous, not anonymous.
  • Authentication information. Rewards device tokens are stored using the App's secure-storage mechanism. Token hashes and expiry information are maintained by the API.
  • Referral information. This includes referral codes, installation attribution obtained through Google Play when available, links between inviter and invitee devices, and referral qualification records.
  • Qualifying-save information. This includes content hashes, media type, source application category, file size and activity timestamps. It is used to calculate task progress and detect duplicate saves.
  • Campaign and reward records. This includes offers joined, task progress, earned rewards, recurring eligibility and redemption history.
  • Redemption information. When you claim mobile data, we process the destination phone number, selected network, reward or bundle, transaction references, processing messages and delivery status.
  • Phone-number reservation information. We retain a phone-number hash, limited number details and its association with a rewards device to prevent the same number being reserved by different devices. Full destination numbers are also processed in redemption records.
  • Connection and country information. Our servers and hosting infrastructure receive your IP address and technical request information. Operational logs may contain timestamps and error information. We use an IP-country database to estimate your country for regional eligibility.
  • Support correspondence. If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you include in your message.

Statusly does not request your WhatsApp password, private chat messages, contacts, bank-card details or GPS location for these features. A phone number is required when requesting delivery of a mobile-data reward.

5. Local Storage and App Preferences

Statusly stores settings such as your selected language, onboarding status, selected WhatsApp source and folder organization on your device.

Rewards authentication tokens use the App's secure-storage mechanism. Availability results and other information may be cached temporarily in memory to reduce repeated requests.

Server-side device records are separate from local preferences and can remain after the App is uninstalled.

6. Saved Photos and Videos

Saved photos and videos are stored on your device using Android's storage system. Statusly does not keep a server-side copy of saved status media as part of its normal operation.

Saved files remain until you delete them through Statusly, your gallery, a file manager or other available tools.

7. Sharing Information With Third Parties

Statusly does not sell, rent or trade your personal information.

Hosting and infrastructure providers process information needed to operate and store API records.

When you claim mobile data, relevant details, including the destination phone number, network, selected bundle and transaction reference, are sent to VTpass and relevant fulfilment providers or mobile networks to process delivery and check transaction status.

If you choose to share media or an app link, Android passes your selected content to the application or service you choose. Referral links contain a referral code. External services handle information under their own policies.

Information may also be disclosed where necessary to comply with applicable legal obligations or valid legal requests.

8. Third-Party Services and Country Detection

Statusly uses third-party libraries and services for functions including Google Play installation attribution, app updates, hosting and VTpass reward fulfilment.

Country detection uses a DB-IP database stored on our server. This lookup does not send each visitor's IP address to an external DB-IP lookup API.

IP Geolocation by DB-IP. DB-IP Lite data is licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International .

IP-country detection is approximate. It does not establish nationality or exact physical location. VPNs, travel and network routing can affect the result.

Service providers may process information on infrastructure outside your country. Their involvement depends on the feature used.

9. Advertising

This version of Statusly does not include AdMob or display third-party advertising.

In-app reward announcements promote available Statusly campaigns. If third-party advertising is introduced later, we will update this policy and implement applicable disclosures and consent requirements.

10. Data Security

We use access controls, device authentication and hashed identifiers to help protect rewards information. Hashed device identifiers and phone-number hashes remain subject to privacy protections.

Public deployment of the rewards API requires HTTPS. Development and sandbox testing configurations are separate from the public service.

No electronic storage system or transmission method is completely secure. Use your device's security features to protect your saved files and app access.

11. Data Retention and Deletion

Server-side rewards profiles and participation records support task progress, referral qualification, reward delivery, support and fraud prevention. They are retained while needed for those purposes.

Uninstalling Statusly or clearing app data may remove local settings and authentication information, depending on Android and backup settings. It does not automatically delete server-side rewards records or make a previously recognized device eligible as a new referral.

You can request deletion of server-side rewards information by emailing iszifyaws@gmail.com. We may need limited information to verify that the request concerns your device or transactions. Do not send passwords or authentication tokens.

Limited transaction records, device identifiers or phone-number reservation records may need to be retained for fraud prevention, disputes or applicable legal obligations. Where retention is necessary, we will explain the relevant reason when responding to your request. Information should not be retained longer than necessary for its applicable purpose.

Saved media can be deleted separately using Statusly, your gallery or file-management tools.

12. Children's Privacy

Statusly is not specifically designed for children. Rewards processing can include device identifiers and activity information.

If you believe a child has provided information that should not have been collected, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.
